Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Application: PharmaCode — B.Pharm NEP 2020 Study Companion
Package ID: com.pharmacode.bpharm | Last Updated: September 2026

PharmaCode ("we", "our", or "us") provides open-access academic learning resources, syllabus references aligned with PCI NEP 2020, and the PharmaHelper AI tutor. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding user data across our mobile applications and websites (https://pharmacode.vercel.app).

1. Zero Account Registration & No Mandatory Personal Data

PharmaCode does not require registration, login credentials, passwords, phone numbers, or credit cards to view syllabus guides, read pharmaceutical notes, or download PDFs. All core educational study resources are freely available without paywalls.

2. Permissions Requested by the Android App

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Strictly used to retrieve online notes, syllabus updates, and query the PharmaHelper AI academic engine.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used optionally to provide daily study reminders and university exam notices. You can disable notifications anytime in your Android settings.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used for on-device study reminder scheduling.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

To support free hosting and maintain application stability, we utilize trusted industry-standard SDKs:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): May display non-invasive banner or interstitial ads. Google AdMob may collect and process anonymous Advertising IDs in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Collects anonymized crash logs and device models to diagnose software bugs and optimize performance across low-RAM devices.
  • AI Inference APIs (Groq, Google Gemini, NVIDIA NIM): Chat queries sent to PharmaHelper are transmitted securely via TLS 1.3 encryption solely for generating academic answers. Conversations are stored strictly on-device in local sandbox storage.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies, users have complete control over their data:

  • All local chat history, bookmarks, and offline notes can be deleted at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings > Apps > PharmaCode > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • To request deletion of any support email inquiries, contact us at the developer email below.

5. Children's Privacy

PharmaCode is designed for undergraduate pharmacy college students and educational professionals. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
