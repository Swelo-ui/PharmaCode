Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Application: PharmaCode — B.Pharm NEP 2020 Study Companion
Package ID: com.pharmacode.bpharm | Category: Education
Developer Entity: PharmaCode Team | Effective Date: September 2026

PharmaCode ("we", "our", or "us") provides open-access academic learning materials, Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) NEP 2020 syllabus guides, free downloadable unit notes, GPAT prep kits, and the PharmaHelper AI academic study tutor. This Privacy Policy informs users of our mobile application (Package ID: com.pharmacode.bpharm) and associated web portal (https://pharmacode.vercel.app) regarding the collection, handling, storage, and protection of user data.

1. Zero Mandatory Accounts & Free Educational Access

PharmaCode is built upon a philosophy of open academic education for pharmacy students across India. You are not required to create an account, provide your real name, submit your phone number, or enter any financial/payment information to access our core features. You can browse all 8 semesters of B.Pharm curricula, read unit notes, and download study PDFs completely anonymously.

2. Optional Account Data (Firebase Authentication)

Students may optionally register or log in using Google Sign-In or Email/Password to sync personal bookmarks and study history across devices. In such cases:

  • Collected Data: Basic profile identifiers (Display Name, Email Address, and chosen avatar key).
  • Handling: Authentication is handled securely through Google Firebase Authentication infrastructure. We never store plain text passwords.
  • No Selling of Data: User email addresses are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party commercial marketing brokers.

3. Permissions Requested by the Android Application

In adherence to Google Play's Principle of Least Privilege, PharmaCode requests only minimal permissions strictly required to deliver core educational functions:

Permission Technical Identifier Exact Purpose & Justification
Internet Access android.permission.INTERNET Fetches latest syllabus curriculum updates, unit notes, and queries the PharmaHelper AI study tutor.
Network State android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Monitors connectivity to serve cached offline study content when no internet connection is detected.
Notifications (Optional) android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Sends voluntary daily PCI revision flashcard reminders and semester exam schedule updates. Can be disabled anytime in app settings.
Exact Study Alarms android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Schedules local study reminders and revision timers requested directly by the student.
Storage Handling Android Scoped Storage Saves downloaded PDF notes directly to the device's standard Downloads directory. PharmaCode does NOT request broad external storage access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

4. PharmaHelper AI Study Tutor & Prompt Data Handling

The PharmaCode mobile app features PharmaHelper, an AI tutor designed for pharmacy concept revision and syllabus explanation:

  • Encrypted In-Transit: User chat questions are transmitted strictly over secure TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS connections to enterprise AI inference providers (Groq Inc., Google Gemini, NVIDIA NIM).
  • Purpose Limitation: Prompts are processed strictly in real-time to generate academic explanations and relevant subject references.
  • No Foundation Model Training: Your academic chat prompts are not used by PharmaCode to train or fine-tune public foundation AI models.
  • Local Sandbox Storage: All chat message histories are saved strictly on your device inside the app's private sandbox. You can permanently wipe your entire chat history at any time using the "Clear Chat" feature in the app.

6. Google Play Data Deletion Policy & Procedures

In accordance with Google Play's 2026 User Data and Data Deletion requirements, users maintain total control over their data:

How to Delete Your Data

1. Instant Local Data Deletion (On-Device):

  • To delete chat history: Tap the Clear Chat icon in PharmaHelper.
  • To delete all local data, bookmarks, and cached files: Open your device Settings > Apps > PharmaCode > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage (Clear Data), or uninstall the application.

2. Cloud Account & Server Data Deletion:

  • If you signed up with an account, you can initiate account deletion inside the app Profile screen or by sending an email with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" to: pharmacode.connect@gmail.com.
  • Upon receiving your request, all associated authentication records, bookmarks, and correspondence will be permanently purged from our databases within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy & Families Policy

PharmaCode is designed specifically for undergraduate and postgraduate pharmacy university students (B.Pharm, D.Pharm, M.Pharm, Pharm.D), faculty members, and competitive exam (GPAT/NIPER) aspirants. The application is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly target, solicit, or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that a user under 13 has provided personal information, we will immediately delete it.

8. Data Security Practices

We implement rigorous security safeguards to protect your information:

  • All network communications between the mobile application and our backend/AI servers are encrypted using TLS 1.3.
  • API credentials and sensitive configurations are obfuscated using bitwise XOR encoding and protected with ProGuard/R8 code minification.
  • Local user preferences and bookmarks are confined to Android sandbox storage, isolated from other apps on your device.
